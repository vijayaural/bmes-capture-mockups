iPad Screen B — Queue
Worklist with Order Preview
The iPad queue uses a two-column layout. The left column shows the list of pending orders. The right column shows the selected order's stage summary with a large Open Capture button. This allows the intern to review what is needed before opening the camera, without any extra navigation steps.
Left Panel — Order List
Order Row Tap Loads the order summary in the right panel without navigating away from the queue.
Color Dot Red: all stages pending. Orange: partially complete. Blue: only After pending.
Right Panel — Order Preview
Open Capture Button Navigates to the full Capture screen for the selected order, landing on the first incomplete stage.
Scan QR Code In the header. Opens the camera in QR scan mode to load an order not in the queue.

iPad Screen I — Capture, WIP Active
Two-Column Capture Layout — Optimized for iPad
The iPad capture screen places all contextual information in the left panel and dedicates the right panel entirely to the camera. The technician can see the SO details, the Before thumbnails for reference, and the full capture log without leaving the camera view. The large shutter button is easy to tap while the other hand positions the device being photographed.
Left Panel Controls
Before Thumbnails Tap any thumbnail to open Photo Detail view (Screen H). Left panel updates; camera stays visible on the right.
Upload from Gallery Opens the iPad photo library. Selected photos are pushed to Acumatica under the WIP stage.
Stage Tabs Switch between Before, WIP, After. Camera stays open; stage badge updates to reflect the new active stage.
Back to Queue Returns to the Queue screen. Camera is closed. Any captured photos are already saved in Acumatica.
Right Panel Controls
Shutter Button Takes the photo immediately. Photo is pushed to Acumatica in the background. Left panel thumbnail count updates. Camera stays open for the next shot.
Flip Camera Switches between rear and front camera. Rear camera is default for device photography.
Scan New Order In the header. Opens the iPad camera in QR scan mode to load a different order.
